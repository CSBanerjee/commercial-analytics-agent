# Commercial Sales KPI Guide

## 1. Actual Sales
Definition: Total revenue generated from completed sales.

Calculation:
Sum of actual_sales_usd.

## 2. Sales Target
Definition: Planned sales revenue for a given period.

Calculation:
Sum of sales_target_usd.

## 3. Sales Target Attainment
Definition: Percentage of the sales target achieved.

Calculation:
(Actual Sales / Sales Target) * 100

Interpretation:
- Above 100%: Target exceeded.
- Equal to 100%: Target achieved.
- Below 100%: Target not achieved.

## 4. Sales Gap
Definition: Difference between sales target and actual sales.

Calculation:
Sales Target - Actual Sales

Interpretation:
- Positive: Sales below target.
- Zero: Target achieved.
- Negative: Sales exceeded target.

## 5. Pipeline Value
Definition: Total value of potential sales opportunities.

Calculation:
Sum of pipeline_value_usd.

Important:
Pipeline value represents potential future revenue, not confirmed actual sales.

## 6. Units Sold
Definition: Total number of units sold.

Calculation:
Sum of units_sold.

## 7. Regional Sales Performance
Definition: Sales performance evaluated across geographical regions.

Available regions:
North, South, East, West, Central.

Compare regions using:
- Actual sales
- Sales target
- Attainment percentage
- Sales gap
- Pipeline value

## 8. Product Sales Performance
Definition: Sales performance evaluated across products.

Available products:
- Diagnostic Systems
- Surgical Solutions
- Patient Monitoring

Compare products using:
- Actual sales
- Sales target
- Attainment percentage
- Units sold
- Pipeline value

## 9. Time Dimension
Dataset period: January 2025 to December 2025.

Date column: month

Format: YYYY-MM

Monthly and annual sales analysis can be performed using this field.
